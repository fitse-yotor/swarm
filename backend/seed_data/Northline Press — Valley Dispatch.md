# Valley Dispatch

## Lead

Thousands joined the Kesh Valley Farmers' March, organised by the Riverside Farmers Union. Union chair Marta Sol said members oppose the filling schedule because irrigation allocations have already been cut. The march took place in the Kesh Valley, three days before the Kesh Summit.

## Reporting

Northline journalist Kofi Ode reported that some farmers believe water scarcity is being worsened by upstream decisions. Officials from the Aurelian Ministry of Water met union representatives after the march. One unnamed official claimed the Ministry would suspend the Accord, a statement the Ministry later denied.

## Context

Food security in the valley is closely tied to irrigation from Lake Varn. Borovian officials describe hydropower revenue as essential to the national budget.
