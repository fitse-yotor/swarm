# Varn Basin Commission — Quarterly Briefing, Q3

## Summary

The Varn Basin Commission convened its quarterly session at Port Selin under Commissioner Ada Mwangi. Commissioner Mwangi reaffirmed that the Basin Allocation Accord remains the governing framework for releases from Lake Varn. Delegations from Aurelia, Borovia and Telmar attended the session.

## Reservoir status

The Borovian Energy Authority operates the Varn Dam and reported that Dam Filling Phase II will begin before the dry season. Downstream inflow to the Kesh Valley fell by an estimated 18 percent during the 2025 Dry Season Shortfall. The Institute for Hydro Climate cautioned that inflow estimates carry a wide margin of uncertainty.

## Delegation positions

Minister Elena Kost stated that Aurelia opposes any acceleration of Dam Filling Phase II without a joint monitoring agreement. Director Ilya Varga argued that revenue under the Hydropower Export Tariff depends on completing the filling schedule. Envoy Henrik Lind indicated Telmar would support an Emergency Drought Protocol if releases fall below the Accord minimum.

## Next steps

The Commission agreed to host the Kesh Summit, where Aurelia and Borovia will negotiate the filling timeline. Dr. Tomas Rhee was asked to advise the Commission on a shared monitoring methodology. The VBC Secretariat will circulate draft monitoring terms to member delegations.
