Abstract

This note, authored by Dr. Tomas Rhee, examines inflow variability in the Lake Varn basin. The Institute for Hydro Climate finds that the 2025 Dry Season Shortfall was driven by both rainfall deficit and upstream storage.

Findings

Accelerated filling of the Varn Dam would reduce Kesh Valley inflow during the critical irrigation window. A joint monitoring arrangement could reduce disputes over release data and improve cross-border trust.

Limitations

Gauge data from Borovia was unavailable for two months, limiting confidence in upstream estimates. The model does not account for informal abstraction in the Kesh Valley.
