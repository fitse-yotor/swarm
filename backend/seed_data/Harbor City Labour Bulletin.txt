Overview

The Harbor Transit Workers Guild announced a 48-hour strike affecting the Bayline and Crosstown routes. Guild president Noor Haddad cited unresolved shift-length rules under the Fair Shifts Ordinance.

Negotiations

Harbor Metro Authority chief Daniel Okafor proposed binding arbitration through the City Labour Board. Mayor Rosa Lindqvist said the city would not fund overtime beyond the current transit budget.

Public response

Commuter Alliance spokesperson Jae Min called for a phased service plan during the strike. Local outlet Harbor Ledger reported that ridership complaints doubled in the first week.
