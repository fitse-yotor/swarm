# Case ETH-SEA-001 — Ethiopia’s Quest for Sea Access

**Status:** research-backed seed case + fictional simulation inputs  
**As of:** 1 November 2026  
**Geographic scope:** Ethiopia, Somalia, Somaliland, Eritrea, Djibouti and Türkiye  
**Purpose:** local M2 Swarm case creation, evidence-aware graph extraction, and multi-agent scenario exploration

> **Research integrity:** Named officials and institutions below represent reported public positions at cited dates. All citizen personas, their comments, and future scenario behaviors are **synthetic**. They are not quotations, not scraped social media, not real citizens, not polling data, and not predictions. Policy positions belong only to speakers/institutions for whom evidence is supplied.

## 1. Case question

How has Ethiopia’s pursuit of reliable sea access been presented by officials, interpreted by international and regional governments, reported in the media, and debated across *illustrative* civilian viewpoints? What alternative diplomatic, economic and communication scenarios could emerge?

## 2. Analytical boundaries

- Separate access for trade from ownership, sovereign territory, military bases, or recognition of Somaliland; do not assume these are equivalent.
- Describe Ethiopia’s stated economic interests without asserting a legal entitlement to a particular foreign coastline.
- Record objections grounded in Somali or Eritrean sovereignty and regional security.
- Treat Somaliland’s status as disputed and do not silently resolve it.
- Store actual speeches as sourced paraphrases unless exact wording is independently checked.
- Exclude fabricated endorsements from real officials and institutions.
- Use simulated comments for UX and graph testing, never for public-opinion statistics.

### Speaker and institutional perspectives

**Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed (Ethiopia).** Reported framing: sea access is economically and strategically important; publicly stated preference for dialogue, negotiation and mutual benefit. His remarks evolved across 2023–2026. Sources S01, S07, S10, S11, S12.

**Nebiat Getachew (Ethiopian Ministry of Foreign Affairs spokesperson, at cited date).** Reportedly emphasized peaceful diplomatic efforts and reciprocity / give-and-take. Source S08.

**Somalia’s presidency and foreign ministry.** Rejected the 2024 Ethiopia–Somaliland memorandum on sovereignty grounds; later joined the December 2024 Ankara Declaration acknowledging a potential commercial-access arrangement under Somali sovereign authority. Sources S04, S06, S13.

**Eritrean Ministry of Information.** Expressed concerns over maritime claims, possible coercion and territorial security in September 2025. Source S09.

**Türkiye / President Erdoğan.** Facilitated the Ethiopia–Somalia Ankara Declaration. Source S06.

**Djibouti / Foreign Minister Mahmoud Ali Youssouf (at cited date).** Publicly proposed access to Tadjoura as a possible alternative in August 2024. Source S05.

**Ethiopian News Agency / Addis Media Network / Reuters / AFP Fact Check.** These are publishers or independent reporting channels, not assumed to endorse the positions they cover. AFP fact-checked a misleading claim that a speech threatened force; that allegation should not be presented as a confirmed quote. Sources S02, S11.

## 7. Proposed M2 Swarm simulation design

**Stage 1 — Case Creation & Graph Build:** Import all cited documents, add verified public entities/events, normalize names, attach evidence, then add separately tagged fictional personas.

**Stage 2 — Environment Setup:** Specify country and institution nodes, actors’ publicly evidenced interests, four topic axes (commerce, sovereignty, cooperation, security), and simulated persona attributes. Do not create statements in real officials’ names.

**Stage 3 — Simulate:** Run hypothetical scenarios using abstract policy actions, institutional constraints and fictional civilian reactions. Keep simulations separate from historical events.

**Stage 4 — Report:** Produce sourced event timelines, actor-position comparisons, uncertainty flags, scenario branches and graph metrics. Do not claim to forecast actual government decisions or infer public opinion from invented data.

**Stage 5 — Deep Interaction:** Allow case analysts to query sourced public records, contrast documented positions and ask fictional synthetic personas scenario questions with a persistent synthetic label.

## 8. Test scenarios

1. **Commercial corridor agreement:** Ethiopia and a coastal partner agree on commercial access via transparent leasing and customs arrangements. Explore logistics, costs and consent.
2. **Diplomatic deadlock:** Talks stall; compare different stakeholders’ likely *hypothetical* concerns without asserting their real future behavior.
3. **Regional integration:** Multiple ports and transport corridors are jointly developed. Compare economic tradeoffs.
4. **Information disorder:** A misleading sea-access quote circulates; test source verification, provenance and correction behavior, without generating deceptive fake posts attributed to real actors.

## 9. Required UI filters

Entity type, country, event date, source, documented vs synthetic, relationship type, source credibility category, topic and hypothetical scenario. Display source evidence in a right-side inspector.
