# Gerar Water Institute — Technical Panel Report on Reservoir Operation

> **FICTIONAL TRAINING MATERIAL.** Names, figures and findings are invented for software testing only.

## Summary

The Gerar Water Institute reviewed the Tamsa Release Protocol for the Gerar Basin Authority. Dr. Selam Tadesse presented the findings to the panel. The Tamsa Reservoir held 62 percent of its capacity at the start of the dry season in the modelled year.

## Findings

The Gerar Water Institute found that the minimum release in the Tamsa Release Protocol keeps the Lower Gerar canals supplied in nine of ten modelled years. The Gerar Water Institute found that the same release lowers the annual output of the Tamsa Hydropower Company by roughly eight percent. The Gerar Water Institute reported that a later start to the releases would leave planting-season canals short in dry years.

The Tamsa Monitoring System would measure reservoir level, canal flow and rainfall. The Highland Development Bank funds the Tamsa Monitoring System. The Gerar Regional Water Bureau would receive the readings. The Lower Gerar Irrigation Council would publish the canal flow figures.

## Limits of the analysis

The panel did not model rainfall extremes beyond the historical record. The panel did not assess compensation for the Tamsa Hydropower Company. The Gerar Water Institute recommends that the Gerar Basin Authority review the phase-in schedule before adopting the Tamsa Release Protocol.
