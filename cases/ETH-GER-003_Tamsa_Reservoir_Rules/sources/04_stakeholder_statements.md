# Stakeholder statements (paraphrased)

> **FICTIONAL TRAINING MATERIAL.** These are invented paraphrases, not quotations, and no real organisation is meant.

The Tamsa Hydropower Company said it would accept release limits if a compensation fund covers lost output. The Tamsa Hydropower Company said it depends on stable reservoir levels to supply the Gerar grid.

The Gerar Farmers' Cooperative Union said the Tamsa Release Protocol helps farmers only if the first release comes before planting. The Gerar Farmers' Cooperative Union asked the Gerar Basin Authority to move the phase-in earlier.

The Lower Gerar Irrigation Council said it supports the Tamsa Release Protocol and the Tamsa Monitoring System. The Lower Gerar Irrigation Council depends on the Tamsa Reservoir for canal water.

The Gerar Regional Water Bureau said it will regulate the Tamsa Reservoir under whichever rules the Gerar Basin Authority adopts. The Gerar Regional Water Bureau negotiates with the Tamsa Hydropower Company and the Gerar Farmers' Cooperative Union.

The Gerar Women's Water Users Association said it supports public weekly readings from the Tamsa Monitoring System.
