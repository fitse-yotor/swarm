# Gerar Basin Authority — Briefing on the Tamsa Release Protocol

> **FICTIONAL TRAINING MATERIAL.** Every organisation, person, place and event in this file is invented to test M2 Swarm. Nothing here describes a real institution, a real position or a real event.

## Background

The Tamsa Reservoir lies in the Gerar Basin in the Ethiopian highlands. The Tamsa Hydropower Company operates the Tamsa Reservoir. The Gerar Regional Water Bureau regulates the Tamsa Reservoir. The Lower Gerar Irrigation Council manages the irrigation canals below the Tamsa Reservoir. The Gerar Farmers' Cooperative Union represents about four thousand smallholder farmers in the Lower Gerar valley.

## The proposal

In March the Gerar Basin Authority proposed the Tamsa Release Protocol. The Tamsa Release Protocol sets seasonal water releases from the Tamsa Reservoir. It requires a minimum release during the planting season and limits how far the reservoir may be drawn down in the dry season.

Director Bekele Mamo leads the Gerar Basin Authority. The Federal Water Ministry supports the Tamsa Release Protocol. The Highland Development Bank funds the Tamsa Monitoring System. The Gerar Water Institute designed the Tamsa Monitoring System. Dr. Selam Tadesse leads the Gerar Water Institute.

## Positions recorded so far

The Tamsa Hydropower Company opposes the minimum release rule in the Tamsa Release Protocol, because lower reservoir levels reduce electricity output. The Tamsa Hydropower Company supports the Tamsa Monitoring System. The Lower Gerar Irrigation Council supports the Tamsa Release Protocol. The Gerar Farmers' Cooperative Union opposes the phase-in schedule of the Tamsa Release Protocol, because the first guaranteed release arrives after planting begins.

The Gerar Regional Water Bureau negotiates with the Tamsa Hydropower Company. The Gerar Water Institute advises the Gerar Basin Authority. The Gerar Women's Water Users Association supports the Tamsa Monitoring System.

## Open questions

- Who pays if reservoir output falls during a dry season?
- Whether the Tamsa Monitoring System will publish readings every week or every month.
- Whether the phase-in of the Tamsa Release Protocol can start before planting.
